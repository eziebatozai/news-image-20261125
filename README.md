## Stories Covered (25 November 2026)

1. **Ukraine — Gulf of Finland Ship Strike**: President Zelenskiy confirms Ukraine carried out the drone strike on a Russian cargo ship; Norway urges caution.
2. **Middle East — Riyadh PIF Tower Strike**: Houthi fighters strike Riyadh's tallest tower for the second time in less than a week, wounding 11; the Saudi-led coalition vows a decisive response.
3. **US–China — Huawei Convicted**: A Brooklyn federal jury convicts Huawei and three subsidiaries on 10 of 12 counts, including fraud and Iran sanctions violations.
4. **US Politics — Presidential Medal of Freedom**: President Trump awards Rudy Giuliani the Presidential Medal of Freedom.
5. **Iraq — Kurdish Oilfields**: Iraq's premier orders federal forces to seize oilfields and crossings in Kurdish-run regions.
6. **Europe — NATO AWACS in Poland**: NATO plans to deploy early-warning aircraft in Poland following reports of missiles landing in the country during an attack on Ukraine.
7. **France — Sarkozy Corruption Trial**: French officials order a corruption case involving former President Nicolas Sarkozy and Carla Bruni to trial.
8. **Sport — Champions League**: Real Madrid overturn a deficit to defeat PSV Eindhoven 5–1 with a Bellingham stunner and an Mbappe brace.

## Usage

Requires Node 22+ (standard library only):

```bash
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

When `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` is not set, `main.ts` writes `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.
